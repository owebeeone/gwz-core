**P2-4** · amendment TR1.8 "The trigger" (line 157) and OD16 (line 356) · The zone-mapped URL is undefined under a redirect, and the cross-reference points the wrong way. The transport follows one validated discovery redirect that may change the host (`gwz-core/src/git/endpoint/https_destination.rs:95-108`; `validate_url_shape` checks scheme and shape, not host identity). The trigger says "the URL maps to the Local Machine, Intranet or Trusted zone … the bound libgit2's own fallback applies (`winhttp.c:230-282`)"; libgit2's fallback maps `t->owner->url`, the configured URL (`winhttp.c:1243-1246` passes `t->owner->url` to `acquire_credentials`), not the redirected one. Read that way, an Intranet-zone configured URL that redirects to an Internet host passes the bound and the SSPI exchange sends the logon session's NetNTLMv2 to the Internet host. *Fix:* the trigger reads "the URL of the request the 401 answered, after the transport's one validated redirect, maps to …", adds "a redirect after the challenge ends the exchange with no credential sent", and notes that libgit2 maps the configured URL; TR1.8's `Negotiate` fixture gains a row: the Intranet-zone name redirecting to the Internet-zone name, refused.
