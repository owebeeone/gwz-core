At that moment no confirmation can be open (the retry machine left Healthy only through a `hi = 0`/`k = 1` failure, which closes any confirmation) and no probe can be in flight (`Connected = 0` before the retest). *Inside:* the probe timer is suspended and test-pending is not entered, so the only holds that can arise are a step's confirmation (on a Suspect) and a `Retry-After` hold (on a Throttle). *Against the confirmation:* a Suspect refusal in a step opens one; its hold stops new starts (the step's are already admitted); quiet comes when the step's wave resolves; `k = Connected + 1`; fair refusal → Overload → exit 1; refuted → the step completes with a refusal and no Overload → exit 2; unfair → re-armed (bounded by churn, as everywhere). *Against Down:* if every connection dies again mid-step, the step's refusals are Suspects → confirmation → quiet with `Connected = 0` → `k = 1` → retry machine counted → confirmation closes; the step has completed with a refusal → exit 2 to STABLE; the retry machine then takes the key to Waiting and, if it persists, Down; a later retest success re-enters RESTORING. *Against a hold:* a Throttle in a step is conclusive iff `hi < S` → Overload → exit 1; inconclusive → the hold stops the step's unstarted admissions (none) → the step completes with a refusal → exit 2. *Against the `Ns` test:* "the only test that can run is a confirmation opened by a step's refusal" excludes it during RESTORING; it waits for the exit, bounded by the step. ## 1. Findings

### [P3-12] The idle discard at hold end needs a pool operation that §4.9 and D11 do not name; routed through eviction it would serialise on the settling hold

- **Location:** §4.5 line 214 ("the key's idle connections are discarded rather than leased (closed, then Settling)"); §4.9 lines 338–341 (the pool's additions: "numbers only", `set_limit`, `set_settle`); D11 line 34 ("a numeric limit … and a numeric settle hold").
- **Root cause:** the discard is specified as an effect without an actor. The pool owns idle entries and exposes no bulk "close this key's idle connections" (C2/C6: `start_closing` is `pub(super)`, `lifecycle.rs:44`; the public closers are eviction for one waiting request, `IdleExpired`, `retire_https_scope` for one HTTPS scope, and `install_capacity` surplus, which `can_install_capacity` refuses while anything is leased, `machine.rs:130-137`).
- **Violated invariant:** D11's statement of what the pool gains is incomplete; an implementer following §4.9 has only eviction to discard with.
- **Sequence:** hold of 10 s ends; 10 idle of identity A, 22 leased, `N = 32` (STABLE after the Overload that set the hold, say `N = 32 → 30`). Members of identity B arrive. If the discard is done through eviction: R1 evicts I1 → settling hold bound to R1 → R1 waits `Ts` → served; R2 evicts I2 → `Ts` → … each new member pays one `Ts` in sequence and the ten dead idle connections leave over `10 × Ts` (≥ 2.5 s), with every member's start serialised behind one hold. If instead the discard is a bulk close at hold end: all ten go Closing at once, settle concurrently, and the next starts wait one `Ts`. The text's "discarded rather than leased" intends the latter. Members of identity A (who could lease) are unaffected either way, except that in the eviction reading they would lease a dead idle connection and fail (F14), which is the very outcome the correction exists to prevent.
- **Impact:** throughput only (`K × Ts` instead of `Ts`), or, in the worst reading, the recurrence of the dead-lease failure P2-13 was fixing for same-identity members. Bounded; not architectural.
- **Required correction:** add to §4.9 a third pool operation, `discard_idle(key)`: closes every `Idle` entry of the key at once (`start_closing(Discarded)`, no waiting request involved), each then disposed by the host and acknowledged by `closed()`, which creates evictor-less settling holds outside SATURATED; and amend D11 ("… a numeric settle hold, and one bulk discard of a key's idle connections"). State that it never goes through eviction and that leases are refused for the key's idle entries between the hold's end and the discard (so a lease on another thread cannot win the race, C8).
- **Closure test:** pool unit test: 10 idle + 22 leased, `discard_idle(key)`: 10 `Closing(Discarded)` at once, 10 holds after `closed()`, no `Evicted`, and a waiting request of another identity becomes `Opening` at `closed + Ts`, not `closed + 10 × Ts`; case 51 extended to assert the elapsed time between the hold's end and the first new discovery is at most `Ts` plus one setup.
