## New findings

**N1 · P3 · §3.20, the §5 Cold replacement, "however it left" · The Healthy exit now conflicts with §5's Healthy→Degraded rule.** My P3-2 wording was the cause. The clause says that for wave setups still in flight after the key left Cold, "however it left", "a retriable failure counts no attempt". But when the key left Cold for **Healthy** (a success before any failure), §5's unchanged rule for Healthy is that the first retriable failure is counted and moves the key to Degraded, and only setups "already in flight when the key left Healthy" are uncounted. The two rules now disagree on the first wave failure after an early success. The consequence is bounded: under the new clause the failed members return to a Healthy key's queue and re-open under normal allocation, so a host that admits one connection and drops the rest receives one extra uncounted parallel wave before the single-probe rules bite; under §5's rule it receives none. TR2.1's implementer must pick one, and no S3.1 row pins it.
*Fix:* "Setups of the wave still in flight once the key has left Cold for Degraded or Closed complete as Degraded's in-flight setups do: …" and, after the Healthy sentence: "The wave's setups still in flight are then Healthy's own, and §5's Healthy and Degraded rules govern them."

**N2 · P3 · §3.10 line 263 against §3.20's new sketch edge · TR8.1's precondition differs between prose and sketch.** Line 263 still reads "TR8.1 (1.1.0) applies, measured with the CLI in process, after TR2.9 and TR2.10 have merged", and line 265 "TR8.4 runs beside TR8.1 (1.1.0), before S5.4"; the fix added "TR2.1 ── TR8.1 (1.1.0) and TR8.4" to the sketch only. A reader of §3.10 alone can still measure TR8.1 before TR2.1, which is the case P3-3 was raised to prevent.
*Fix:* line 263 reads "after TR2.1, TR2.9 and TR2.10 have merged"; line 265 gains "after TR2.1".
