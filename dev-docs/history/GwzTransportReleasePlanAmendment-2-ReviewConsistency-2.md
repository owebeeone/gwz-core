**Required correction.** Add to the trigger: "and the challenge offers no scheme the transport's gh route serves; when it does, the gh route is tried first and OD16 fires only on its failure", or state explicitly that TR1.8 decides the precedence and S7.2 (1.1.0)'s notes record it.
