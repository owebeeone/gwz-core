## 1. Findings

### [P3-1] "Where parity is not exact" presents its first two cases as inherent, although they follow from C10's unresolved choice; N14 then claims "as in gwz 1.0" for a skip 1.0.17 did not make; and the draft does not say who decides C10

- **Location:** §11 L300–302 ("Where parity is not exact … The transport answers `Basic`, or reports M2 or M6"; the NTLM-only case "reports M6"); C10 (L246: "it is a wire-format decision for the lane owner"); N14 (L364: "On clone, as in gwz 1.0, a `private: true` repository is skipped quietly when … the server asked for authentication gwz cannot answer"); §8 (L173: "D19 keeps 1.0.17's codes, and changes only … on clone, a few members 1.0.17 failed, which are now skipped").
- **Violated invariant:** F1's "parity first … make revision 3 match it", and the draft's own §11 principle "Codes, and clone's skip or failure, are 1.0.17's wherever 1.0.17 had the case". 1.0.17 had both cases: a `Negotiate`-only (or `NTLM`-only with a helper) challenge on macOS/Linux ended in "'Negotiate' authentication is not supported" (`httpclient.c:527-531`), a generic error `clone_error` does not recognize (`transport.rs:768-793` at `v1.0.17`), so the member failed loudly with `git_command_failed`. The draft's reason the transport cannot match this is that the offered schemes do not reach the core — which is exactly C10's option 1 (the `Failure` detail carrying M6's scheme names, and for the "`Basic` offered too, no helper answered" sub-case, M2's). Case 3 (M8's control-character and colon causes) is genuinely inherent under D17 and is not at issue.
- **Reproduction:** read L300–302 as a reader who then reads C10's option 1: with scheme names crossing, clone's boundary can fail a `Negotiate`-only or `NTLM`-only member loudly as 1.0.17 did and skip a `Bearer`-only one as 1.0.17 did; the text says the deviation is what "the transport" does, not what one option of C10 does. Then read N14: it tells the user the M6 skip is "as in gwz 1.0", which L301–302 contradict for those schemes.
- **Impact:** the operator, asked under F1's parity rule, sees a parity exception stated as unavoidable when a pending wire-format choice removes it; the user's note states parity where §11 admits none. C10's decider is also inconsistent: the draft says the lane owner, the lane owner's brief says the operator, and §12 has no question for it.
- **Required correction:** L300–302 read "Under C10's option 2 (no detail), …"; under option 1 the clone boundary fails these members as 1.0.17 did, and the text says so (or C10 is resolved to option 1 under F1 and the two cases leave the list). N14's clause reads "when … the server asked for authentication gwz cannot answer (gwz 1.0 failed some of these on clone; see §11)" or drops "as in gwz 1.0" for that item under option 2. C10 states who decides it, and if the operator, becomes OQ7 with (1) the detail and (2) shared text as its options.
- **Closure test:** §11's exception list and N14 agree under each C10 option; §12 or C10 names the decider.
